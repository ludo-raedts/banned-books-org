## Findings (patterns seen, nothing applied)

1. Publication year as ban year. Several older rows carry the book's publication year as year_started (A Farewell to Arms 1929, Howl 1955, How to Kill 1973, The Turner Diaries Germany 1979). The same pattern as source 825 earlier today, in rows from "Wikipedia" and "List of books banned by governments" sources.
2. Russian extremist register, early entries. Entries around #510–#588 all carry year_started 2020 ("Added to list: 2020-09-04") although their court decisions date from 2009. The feed date was taken for the listing date.
3. New Zealand rows from FYI.org.nz. The note template says "classified as objectionable" for every title; for at least American Psycho and The Story of O the decision was a restriction (R18, professionals only), not a ban.
4. Mat-Su 2020. The rescinded board vote covered five books; only The Great Gatsby was in this set. The rows for The Things They Carried, I Know Why the Caged Bird Sings, Catch-22 and Invisible Man, if present, need the same change.
5. Duplicate ban. The Satanic Verses has two Malaysia rows for the same ban (#1003 from Wikipedia, #26694 from the KDN register).
6. PEN "banned pending investigation". Rows from PEN's index stay active after a district reviews a book and keeps it (New Kid; probably Gabi, a Girl in Pieces and All American Boys). PEN records the removal, not the outcome.
