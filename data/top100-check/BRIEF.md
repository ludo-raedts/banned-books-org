The rows in data/top100-check/rows.json (fields ban_id, slug, title, country, year, status, note, first_published_year) cite the Wikipedia "List of books banned by governments" but that list does NOT visibly record this exact country+book (automatic text match failed; the match is crude, so naming differences are possible).
For each of your ban_ids (slice file), establish with sources YOU ACTUALLY OPENED (WebFetch the page; search-result snippets alone do not count):
- whether the book was banned/censored in that country, by whom/how, in what year, and whether it is still in force;
- the best specific source URL (government/court/official list, major news, scholarly source, the book's or author's Wikipedia article citing a source — say what that article cites).
Hard rules: (1) list every URL you opened in `opened_urls`; a verdict other than UNCHECKED requires at least one opened page that says it. (3) Missing evidence is never evidence of falsity. (4) Paraphrase; no long quotes. (5) Do not guess URLs.
Output JSON array to your findings file: {ban_id, slug, country, our_year, verdict, source_url, source_name, locator, year_found, date_type, status_now, correction, suggested_note (<=300 chars, only what the opened source records, or null), opened_urls[], uncertainty}.
