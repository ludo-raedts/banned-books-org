## Sourced context material (books with no context text)
- **#944 The Land of Spices** — Censorship Board listed it 6 May 1941 (p. 209); secret deliberations, no reasons; 1942 Seanad no-confidence motion by Sir John Keane (defeated 34–2) used it as an example; 1946 Act created an appeals board; O'Brien appealed successfully, the only author to appeal a ban on her own work (pp. 209–210). Her *Mary Lavelle* (1936) appeal was denied.
- **#14306 Jenny Lives with Eric and Martin** — Danish original 1981, English 1983; The Sun 6 May 1986; Section 28 (Local Government Act 1988) repealed 2000 Scotland / 2003 England+Wales; ILEA removed it 1987 (pp. 306–313). No contradiction with our GB 1986–2003 row.
- **#498 Persepolis** — Chicago CPS March 2013: removed from 7th-grade curriculum, library retention; CEO said "not banned" (pp. 369–372). No BANS row exists for it.
